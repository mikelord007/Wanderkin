## Objective
Replace placeholder-quality procedural biome props/structures with a polished, cohesive, stylized miniature-world environment system: shared biome framework, variant asset families (trees/palms, bushes, cacti, rocks, walls/cliffs, dressing), composition-based placement, grounding, lighting/material polish, Tropical and Desert upgraded, future biomes data-driven. Stylized, not photoreal. Performance and gameplay readability preserved. Uploaded surface stays recognisable.

## Non-negotiable invariants (from the shipped biome feature; all workers)
- Decorative props NEVER join physics. Only generated structures (adventure-* boxes, planner helpers) collide, and their colliders/dimensions/positions must not change. Visual shells for walls/cliffs/steps must stay within the collider footprint plus a small bevel tolerance and never reduce the walkable top.
- Placement safety contract: geometry reserves a prop footprint of `PROP_UNIT_RADIUS[kind] * height + 0.02`; the renderer may only shrink, never exceed, that footprint (crowns, fronds, clusters included). Route/objective/spawn exclusions, support rays, edge margins, water safety and the 400k analysis cap are unchanged unless the geometry owner changes them deliberately with tests.
- `prepareBiomeLayout` stays deterministic for a seed; theme is independent of gameplay geometry; Original restores byte-identically (no props, no patches, no lighting change); repeated switching does not accumulate resources (single-owner dispose, idempotent).
- Reduced-effects budgets remain meaningful; draw-call and instance budgets are stated per biome; no large polygon inflation. Cartoon per-channel posterisation must still read well (avoid fine noisy detail).
- No paid/provider calls; any GLB assets are hand-authored or generated offline by our own scripts, self-hosted, licence noted. No third-party model packs without orchestrator approval.
- User owns hands-on play; agents do narrow automated checks plus real-Chrome screenshots from the normal gameplay camera.
- Commits: Nimbalyst `developer_git_commit_proposal` only, scoped paths, per coherent milestone.
